Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

How long does a typical repair take?

Most screen and battery replacements are completed within 30–60 minutes. More complex repairs like water damage recovery or motherboard work may take 24–48 hours. We'll give you an accurate time estimate when you drop off your device.

Do you offer a warranty on repairs?

Yes — all repairs come with a 90-day warranty covering parts and labour. If the same issue returns within that period, we'll fix it at no additional cost. The warranty does not cover new physical damage or unrelated issues.

What brands do you repair?

We repair all major brands including Apple (iPhone, iPad, MacBook), Samsung, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Realme, Oppo, Vivo, Lenovo, HP, Dell, and most other Android phones and Windows laptops. Not sure about your device? Just call us.

Is the diagnostic really free?

Absolutely. Bring in your device and we'll assess the problem at no charge. You'll receive a clear estimate before any work begins — you're never obligated to proceed.

Will I lose my data during the repair?

Standard repairs like screen or battery replacement do not affect your data. For complex repairs that may require resetting the device, we'll always warn you in advance and suggest backing up first. We take data privacy seriously.

Do you use original parts?

We use OEM-compatible quality parts that meet or exceed manufacturer standards. For Apple devices, we offer both standard and premium OEM options. We'll always tell you exactly which parts we're using before the repair.
